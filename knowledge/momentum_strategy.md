# MOMENTUM STRATEGY

## DEFINITION
Buy assets that have outperformed peers over 3-12 months. Momentum is one of the most persistent anomalies in finance, documented across 300+ years of data and every major market.

## WHY IT WORKS
- **Underreaction**: Investors slowly incorporate new information, causing trends
- **Herding**: Institutional money flows create sustained price pressure
- **Anchoring**: Sellers hold losers too long, buyers chase winners
- **Disposition effect**: Investors sell winners early, hold losers — extends trends

## SIGNALS
- **Relative Strength**: stock's return vs sector/index over 3-6 months
- **New Highs**: stock making 52-week or all-time highs
- **Volume Expansion**: volume rising with price (conviction)
- **52-week high proximity**: within 5% of 52-week high
- **Moving average stack**: price > 50DMA > 200DMA

## ENTRY RULES
1. Stock is in top 20% by 12-month return (excluding most recent month — "12-1 momentum")
2. Stock is above 200DMA
3. Stock has outperformed NIFTY by 5%+ over 3 months
4. Volume on last up-day was above 20-day average
5. RSI between 50-70 (strong but not overbought)

## EXIT RULES
- Stop-loss: 2x ATR below entry (or 10% below 52-week high, whichever is closer)
- Target: trail stop as stock makes new highs
- Time exit: if no new high in 20 trading days, exit
- Death cross: 50DMA crosses below 200DMA = immediate exit

## POSITION SIZING
- 1% risk per trade
- Max 5 momentum positions simultaneously
- Equal weight (don't concentrate in one stock)

## BACKTESTED EDGE
- Historical outperformance: 2-4% annually over buy-and-hold
- Works best in trending markets
- Fails in ranging markets (whipsaw)
- Sharpe ratio: 0.5-0.8 (before costs)

## INDIAN MARKET ADAPTATION
- Use NIFTY 500 as the universe
- Rebalance monthly
- Avoid stocks with < 100cr daily turnover (illiquid momentum fails)
- Be careful around earnings (momentum can reverse on bad results)

## COMMON MISTAKES
1. **Buying after the gap-up** — wait for the first pullback
2. **Holding through a death cross** — exit immediately
3. **Concentrating in one sector** — diversify across 3+ sectors
4. **Ignoring volume** — momentum without volume is suspect
5. **Trading momentum in a bear market** — momentum fails when the index is below 200DMA
