# EARNINGS ANALYSIS

## DEFINITION
Earnings are the most powerful catalyst for stock prices. A stock can move 10-20% on a single earnings report. The key is not just the numbers — it's the guidance, tone, and context.

## WHAT TO EXTRACT

### Hard Numbers
- **Revenue Growth**: YoY and QoQ (sequential)
- **EPS Growth**: YoY and QoQ
- **Margin Expansion/Contraction**: gross margin, operating margin, net margin
- **Guidance**: raised, lowered, or affirmed
- **Cash Flow**: operating cash flow vs net income (quality of earnings)

### Soft Signals
- **Management Tone**: confident vs cautious (count positive/negative words in Q&A)
- **Analyst Engagement**: depth of questions (top-tier analysts asking = bullish)
- **Hedging Language**: "challenging," "headwinds," "we'll see" = red flags
- **Guidance Quality**: specific numbers vs vague qualitative statements

## EARNINGS SURPRISE

### Beat (positive surprise)
- Stock beats consensus EPS estimate
- Reaction: +3% to +10% on day 1
- Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD): stock continues drifting up for 30-60 days
- Edge: buy the beat, hold for 30 days

### Miss (negative surprise)
- Stock misses consensus EPS estimate
- Reaction: -5% to -15% on day 1
- PEAD works in reverse: stock continues falling for 30-60 days
- Edge: sell/avoid the miss, don't catch the falling knife

## GUIDANCE — MORE IMPORTANT THAN THE BEAT

| Scenario | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|
| Beat + Raise Guidance | +8% to +15% (best case) |
| Beat + Affirm Guidance | +2% to +5% |
| Beat + Lower Guidance | -5% to -10% (trap!) |
| Miss + Raise Guidance | +3% to +8% (recovery signal) |
| Miss + Affirm Guidance | -3% to -8% |
| Miss + Lower Guidance | -10% to -20% (worst case) |

## EARNINGS QUALITY CHECKS

### Red Flags (low-quality earnings)
- Net income >> operating cash flow (accruals anomaly)
- One-time gains inflating EPS
- Revenue recognition changes
- Frequent restructuring charges
- Stock-based compensation rising rapidly
- Receivables growing faster than revenue (channel stuffing)

### Green Flags (high-quality earnings)
- Operating cash flow > net income (cash backing)
- Consistent margin expansion
- Revenue growth from volume, not price hikes
- Guidance raised with specific numbers
- Management buying stock after earnings

## POST-EARNINGS ANNOUNCEMENT DRIFT (PEAD)

### The Anomaly
- Stocks that beat earnings drift upward for 60 days
- Stocks that miss drift downward for 60 days
- The drift is 1-3% on average — small but persistent
- Discovered by Bernard & Thomas (1989), confirmed in 100+ studies

### How to Trade It
1. **Buy the beat**: enter on day 1-2 after the earnings gap
2. **Hold for 30-60 days**: the drift persists
3. **Don't exit on day 1**: the gap is just the beginning
4. **Size normally**: 1% risk, the edge is reliable
5. **Avoid the miss**: don't try to catch falling knives

## INDIAN MARKET SPECIFICS

### Earnings Calendar
- Q1 results: July-August
- Q2 results: October-November
- Q3 results: January-February
- Q4 + Annual: April-May

### Key Metrics for Indian Stocks
- **Revenue growth** > 15% = strong
- **EBITDA margin** expansion = positive
- **PAT margin** > 10% = healthy
- **ROE** > 15% = quality
- **Debt/Equity** < 0.5 = safe

### Indian Earnings Sources
- NSE corporate filings
- Moneycontrol earnings calendar
- BSE results page
- Screener.in (historical financials)

## MANAGEMENT TONE ANALYSIS

### Positive Words (count these)
- strong, growth, robust, record, excellent, ahead, expand, opportunity, confident, momentum

### Negative Words (count these)
- challenging, headwinds, difficult, decline, pressure, weak, soft, cautious, uncertain, macro

### Hedging Phrases (red flags)
- "we'll see"
- "cautiously optimistic"
- "near-term challenges"
- "visibility is low"
- "macro uncertainty"
- "softness in demand"

### Scoring
- Positive:Negative ratio > 3:1 in Q&A = bullish
- Positive:Negative ratio < 1:1 = bearish
- Any hedging phrase = dock 1 point
- Multiple hedging phrases = strong sell signal
