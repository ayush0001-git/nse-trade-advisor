# CRASHES HISTORY

## PURPOSE
Study past crashes to recognize the patterns before they happen again. Markets don't repeat exactly, but they rhyme.

## 2008 GLOBAL FINANCIAL CRISIS

### Timeline
- **Oct 2007**: NIFTY peaks at 6,357
- **Jan 2008**: NIFTY starts declining
- **Sep 2008**: Lehman Brothers collapses
- **Oct 2008**: NIFTY crashes to 2,524 (−60% from peak)
- **Mar 2009**: Final low at 2,539

### Warning Signs (visible 3-6 months before)
- NIFTY below 200DMA (broke in Jan 2008)
- VIX rising steadily (from 15 to 40+)
- FII continuous net selling
- Breadth deteriorating (< 30% stocks above 50DMA)
- Credit spreads widening
- Housing bubble bursting (US subprime)

### What Worked
- **Cash**: standing aside preserved capital
- **Short selling**: trend-following on the short side made fortunes
- **Defensive sectors**: FMCG, Pharma held up better
- **Gold**: rose as investors fled to safety

### What Failed
- **Buy and hold**: −60% drawdown
- **Averaging down**: "buying the dip" destroyed capital
- **Value traps**: cheap stocks got cheaper
- **Leverage**: wiped out overleveraged traders

### Lessons
1. When NIFTY breaks below 200DMA, raise cash
2. Don't fight the trend — go short or stand aside
3. VIX > 30 = high risk, halve positions
4. Credit market signals precede equity crashes

## 2020 COVID CRASH

### Timeline
- **Feb 2020**: NIFTY at 12,430 (all-time high)
- **Mar 2020**: NIFTY crashes to 7,610 (−39% in 1 month)
- **Mar 2020**: Global central banks intervene
- **Apr 2020**: Recovery begins
- **Dec 2020**: NIFTY back to 14,000 (+84% from low)

### Warning Signs
- VIX spiked from 15 to 80 in 3 weeks
- Global contagion (not just India)
- Liquidity crisis (even Treasuries sold off briefly)
- Economic shutdown (unprecedented)

### What Worked
- **Cash + patience**: buying the March lows made 84% in 9 months
- **Quality stocks**: RELIANCE, TCS recovered fastest
- **Pharma/IT**: defensive sectors led the recovery
- **Trend following**: caught the massive uptrend post-March

### What Failed
- **Panic selling at the bottom**: sold at 7,600, missed the recovery
- **Leverage**: margin calls forced selling at the worst time
- **Concentrated bets in travel/hospitality**: structural damage

### Lessons
1. Crashes can happen in days, not months — have a plan
2. Central bank intervention marks the bottom (Fed, RBI)
3. Recovery is fast — don't stay in cash too long
4. Quality wins in recovery (large caps, strong balance sheets)

## 1992 HARSHAD MEHTA SCAM (India-Specific)

### Timeline
- **1991-92**: Harshad Mehta drives up prices using bank funds
- **Apr 1992**: Scam exposed
- **BSE Sensex crashes** from 4,467 to 2,564 (−43%)

### Pattern
- Driven by fraud (not fundamentals)
- Specific stocks inflated (ACC, Sterlite, Videocon)
- Sudden exposure → panic → crash

### Lessons
1. When something looks too good to be true, it is
2. Fraud-driven rallies reverse 100%+
3. Watch for unusual volume in specific stocks

## 2000 DOT-COM BUBBLE

### Pattern
- Tech stocks inflated on "new economy" narrative
- P/E ratios of 100+ for companies with no earnings
- Nasdaq fell 78% from peak (Mar 2000 to Oct 2002)
- Indian IT stocks (INFY, WIPRO, SATYAM) fell 60-80%

### Lessons
1. "This time is different" is never true
2. Valuations matter — a P/E of 100 is a bubble
3. Sector concentration kills (tech-only portfolios were wiped out)

## COMMON CRASH PATTERNS (Early Warning)

### 1. The Blow-Off Top
- Rapid acceleration in the last 2-4 weeks (15%+ in a month)
- Volume climax (highest volume of the rally)
- Media euphoria ("stocks only go up")
- Retail investors piling in with leverage
- **Action**: Take profits, raise cash, tighten stops

### 2. The Distribution Pattern
- Index makes new high but breadth doesn't confirm
- Fewer stocks participating (NIFTY up but midcaps down)
- Leaders stop leading (the strongest stocks stall)
- **Action**: Reduce exposure, watch for confirmation

### 3. The VIX Spike
- VIX rises 50%+ in a week (from 15 to 25+)
- Usually precedes a 5-10% correction
- **Action**: Halve position sizes, widen stops

### 4. The FII Exodus (India)
- FIIs sell > 2000cr/day for 3+ consecutive days
- NIFTY tends to drop 2-3% within a week
- **Action**: Raise cash to 50%, avoid new longs

### 5. The Credit Signal
- Corporate bond yields spike (credit spread widening)
- Defaults rising (even small ones)
- Usually precedes equity market stress by 2-3 months
- **Action**: Reduce leverage, increase cash
