# MARKET REGIMES

## OBJECTIVE
First answer: "What market are we in?" Then choose the strategy that works in that regime.

---

## BULL MARKET

### Characteristics
- Index above 200DMA
- Rising earnings
- Risk-on sentiment
- FII net buyers
- Broad participation (advance/decline > 2:1)
- Volatility declining (VIX < 15)

### Preferred Strategies
- **Momentum** — buy stocks making new highs
- **Growth** — pay up for high-growth names
- **Breakouts** — buy stocks crossing resistance on volume
- **Relative Strength** — buy stocks outperforming the index
- **Sector Rotation** — rotate into leading sectors

### Risk Management
- Full position sizes allowed
- Trail stops, don't fixed-target
- Hold winners longer

### Indian Context
- NIFTY above 200DMA
- FII net buying > 500cr/day
- INDIA VIX < 15
- Breadth: > 60% stocks above 50DMA

---

## BEAR MARKET

### Characteristics
- Index below 200DMA
- Earnings deterioration
- Risk-off sentiment
- FII net sellers
- Narrow participation
- Volatility rising (VIX > 25)

### Preferred Strategies
- **Short Selling** (intraday or futures)
- **Trend Following** (on the short side)
- **Defensive Sectors** — FMCG, Pharma, IT
- **Cash** — stand aside is a position
- **Mean Reversion** — only for oversold bounces

### Risk Management
- Halve position sizes
- Tighten stops
- No averaging down
- Raise cash to 50%+

### Indian Context
- NIFTY below 200DMA
- FII net selling > 1000cr/day
- INDIA VIX > 25
- Breadth: < 30% stocks above 50DMA

---

## HIGH VOLATILITY

### Characteristics
- Elevated volatility (ATR% > 80th percentile)
- Large daily moves (2%+ range)
- Gap-ups and gap-downs frequent
- VIX rising

### Preferred Strategies
- **Volatility Trading** — sell options when IV > HV
- **Mean Reversion** — fade extremes
- **Reduced Position Sizes** — halve normal size
- **Wider Stops** — 3x ATR instead of 2x

### Risk Management
- Position size = 0.5% risk (not 1%)
- Stop = 3x ATR (wider than normal)
- No pyramiding
- Exit before major events (RBI, Fed, elections)

---

## LOW VOLATILITY

### Characteristics
- Stable trends
- Small daily ranges
- VIX low and stable
- Clear regime (bull or bear)

### Preferred Strategies
- **Trend Following** — ride the trend
- **Momentum** — buy strength
- **Full position sizes** — volatility is calm
- **Standard stops** (2x ATR)

### Risk Management
- Full 1% risk per trade
- Standard 2x ATR stops
- Can pyramid into winners

---

## RANGING / SIDEWAYS

### Characteristics
- ADX < 20
- Price oscillating between support/resistance
- No clear trend
- Choppy, false breakouts

### Preferred Strategies
- **Mean Reversion** — buy support, sell resistance
- **Range Trading** — fade the edges
- **Bollinger Band** strategies
- **Avoid breakouts** — they fail in ranges

### Risk Management
- Smaller positions
- Tight stops at range extremes
- Take profits at opposite end of range

---

## REGIME DETECTION RULES

1. **Check volatility first** — if ATR% is elevated and persistent, regime = VOLATILE
2. **Check trend** — ADX > 25 with +DI > -DI = TRENDING UP; ADX > 25 with -DI > +DI = TRENDING DOWN
3. **Check range** — ADX < 20 = RANGING
4. **Check transition** — ADX 20-25 = UNKNOWN (wait for confirmation)
5. **Index filter** — NIFTY above/below 200DMA confirms bull/bear
