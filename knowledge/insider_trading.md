# INSIDER TRADING ANALYSIS

## DEFINITION
Insiders (CEOs, directors, promoters, officers) have material non-public information. Their trading activity is a signal — especially cluster buying (multiple insiders buying simultaneously).

## WHY IT WORKS
- Insiders know more than outsiders
- They buy when they believe the stock is undervalued
- They sell for many reasons (diversification, taxes, lifestyle) — selling is noisy
- But buying is a strong signal: insiders only buy to make money

## SIGNAL TYPES

### Insider Buying (BULLISH)
- **CEO/CFO buying**: strongest signal — they know the most
- **Director buying**: strong signal
- **Promoter buying** (India): very strong — they rarely buy unless confident
- **Cluster buying**: 3+ insiders buying within 30 days = strongest signal
- **Open market purchases**: stronger than option exercises (real money)

### Insider Selling (NOISY)
- Single insider selling = weak signal (could be diversification)
- Multiple insiders selling = mild bearish
- CEO selling > 50% of holdings = strong bearish
- Selling after a big run-up = take profits (not necessarily bearish)
- 10b5-1 planned sales = no signal (pre-scheduled)

## SCORING

### Heavy Buying (Bullish +3)
- 3+ insiders buying in 30 days
- CEO buying with personal funds
- Total insider buying > 1% of shares outstanding
- Buying at market price (not option exercise)

### Moderate Buying (Bullish +1)
- 1-2 insiders buying
- Director-level purchases
- Small relative to shares outstanding

### No Activity (Neutral 0)
- No recent insider transactions
- Or balanced buying and selling

### Moderate Selling (Bearish -1)
- 1-2 insiders selling
- Small relative to holdings
- Some option exercises

### Heavy Selling (Bearish -3)
- 3+ insiders selling in 30 days
- CEO selling > 25% of holdings
- Selling at market price (not 10b5-1)

## INDIAN MARKET SPECIFICS

### Promoter Activity
- **Promoter buying**: VERY bullish — promoters rarely buy unless very confident
- **Promoter pledging**: BEARISH — pledged shares = financial stress
- **Promoter stake increase**: bullish (via preferential allotment or open market)
- **Promoter stake decrease**: bearish (especially if below 50%)

### Sources (India)
- NSE insider trading filings (daily)
- BSE insider dealings
- Moneycontrol insider trading page
- Watch for "Persons Acting in Concert" (PAC) filings

### Filing Timeline
- Insiders must file within 2 trading days of transaction (SEBI)
- Form: FORM-D (India)
- Delay in filing = red flag

## RED FLAGS
- **Promoter pledging increasing**: financial stress, potential forced selling
- **Insider selling before bad news**: illegal but happens — watch the pattern
- **Auditor change + insider selling**: massive red flag
- **Promoter reducing stake below 25%**: loss of control, bearish

## GREEN FLAGS
- **Promoter increasing stake via open market**: strongest bullish signal in India
- **Employee ESOP trust buying in open market**: company supporting stock price
- **Buyback announcement + insider buying**: double confirmation
- **Promoter buying after a big correction**: confidence in fundamentals

## COMBINATION SIGNALS

### Strong Buy (insider component)
- Promoter buying + CEO buying + cluster buying
- No pledging
- Stake increasing

### Strong Sell (insider component)
- Promoter selling + CEO selling + cluster selling
- Pledging increasing
- Stake decreasing below 50%
