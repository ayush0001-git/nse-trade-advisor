How to read these results

Strategy Backtest

  • Avg Ret: Mean return across 10 stocks over 5 years.
  • Alpha: Strategy return minus buy-and-hold return. Positive = strategy beat holding the stock.
  • Win%: Percentage of trades that were profitable.
  • PF: Profit factor = sum of wins / sum of losses. >1 = profitable.
  • Trades: Average number of trades per stock over the period.

Sector Rotation

  • Momentum: Weighted avg of 1m/3m/6m returns. Higher = stronger sector.
  • Bias: BULLISH (top 1/3), NEUTRAL (middle), BEARISH (bottom 1/3).
  • Use: Favor longs in BULLISH sectors, avoid new longs in BEARISH sectors.
