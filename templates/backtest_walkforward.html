Walk-forward · {{ symbol }}

Rolling 1-year train / 3-month test windows across 5 years of daily bars. Each fold picks the Sharpe-best (RSI-low, RSI-high, ADX-min) combo in-sample, then evaluates that combo on the next unseen 3 months. The mean out-of-sample Sharpe is the honest score — it tells you whether the optimizer is finding signal or fitting noise (López de Prado's classic overfit trap).
